# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

1. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or bank details with anyone.
2. Do not enter your UPI PIN to receive money – PIN is only required to send money.
3. Always verify payment requests before approving them.
4. Avoid clicking suspicious links received through SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
5. Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts.
6. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
7. Do not share personal information such as address, phone number, or ID proofs publicly.
8. Download apps only from trusted sources like Google Play Store or App Store.
9. Regularly check bank statements for unauthorized transactions.
10. Be cautious of unknown callers claiming to be bank officials or customer care.

If you become a victim of cybercrime, report immediately:

National Cyber Crime Portal:
https://cybercrime.gov.in
