# UPI / Online Payment Fraud Case Study

UPI fraud is one of the fastest growing cybercrimes in India due to the rapid increase in digital payments through apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm. In this type of fraud, scammers trick individuals into authorizing payments or sharing sensitive details such as OTP, debit card information, or UPI PIN.

A common real-life example involves fraudsters posing as customer care executives or buyers from online marketplaces like OLX. In many reported cases, scammers contacted sellers pretending to purchase an item and sent a fake payment request link or QR code. The victim was told that entering their UPI PIN was required to receive the payment. However, entering the PIN actually approved a payment from the victim’s account to the fraudster.

According to reports published in Indian news, thousands of people lose money every year through such scams. Students, elderly people, and first-time digital payment users are often targeted because they may not fully understand how UPI authorization works.

The consequences include financial loss, mental stress, and compromise of personal financial information. Victims may lose small or large amounts of money depending on the situation.

This case highlights the importance of digital awareness, verifying payment requests carefully, and never entering a UPI PIN to receive money.
