SOCIAL MEDIA DO's

1. Maintain a professional profile
2. Think before posting content
3. Respect others online
4. Protect your personal information
5. Use privacy settings wisely


SOCIAL MEDIA DON'Ts

1. Do not share confidential information
2. Do not post offensive content
3. Do not engage in cyberbullying
4. Do not spread fake news
5. Do not share passwords with anyone
